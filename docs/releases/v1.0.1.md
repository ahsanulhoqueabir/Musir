# Musir 1.0.1

**Version 1.0.1** · Android 7.0 and newer

## What's new

Musir 1.0.1 is the first public release of Musir, a simple, fast music player for the songs
on your Android phone. No accounts, no ads, no internet needed.

## Highlights

- **Your library, automatically**: finds the songs on your phone with titles, artists, albums and cover art.
- **Browse and search**: Songs, Albums, Artists, Favorites, Recently played and Recently added.
- **Now Playing**: shuffle, repeat, 10-second skip, favorites and a queue you can reorder.
- **Playlists**: create, rename, reorder and play them, with Undo if you delete one by mistake.
- **Background playback**: controls on the notification, lock screen, headset and Bluetooth.
- **New look**: a new Musir logo, app icon and splash screen, softer colors, and light and dark themes.
- **Picks up where you left off**: reopens your last queue and position (can be turned off in Settings).

## Fixes

- Albums with a guest artist on one song are no longer split into two albums.
- Fixed a crash when closing the Privacy dialog in Settings.
- The mini-player, player controls and home search bar now fit properly on small screens and with large text.

## Download

Download one APK from the **Assets** section below.

| File                          | Choose it if                                                        |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `app-arm64-v8a-release.apk`   | You have a modern phone or tablet (**recommended for most people**) |
| `app-release.apk`             | You're not sure (universal, works everywhere, larger download)      |
| `app-armeabi-v7a-release.apk` | You have an older or budget 32-bit phone                            |
| `app-x86_64-release.apk`      | You're using an emulator or certain Chromebooks                     |

## How to install

1. Download your APK on your phone and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager (**Install unknown apps**).
   This is normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.
4. Allow access to **Music and audio** so Musir can find your songs.

Need help? See the [user guide](https://github.com/ahsanulhoqueabir/Musir#readme).
